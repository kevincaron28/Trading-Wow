# Goblin Ledger: store search research

27 November 2026. Follows a Testers Community ASO report written for the owner's other app (Guilded phone): its checks
(keyword in title, short description and first paragraph; 3,000+ characters; bullet sections; benefits first; no
promotional claims) are applied to Goblin Ledger. The listing text is in [../PLAY_STORE.md](../PLAY_STORE.md).

## Keywords

**Primary:** `wow auction house prices` (French: `prix hôtel des ventes WoW`).
**Secondary:** `wow forever auction house`, `wow ah price tracker`.

Evidence, with its limits: no search-volume figures were available. This is Google's autocomplete (US English, and
Canadian French) and the names Google Play's own search returned on 27 November.

| Typed | Suggested, in order |
| --- | --- |
| wow auction house | wow auction house, ... forever, ... prices (then price checker, app, price tracker) |
| wow auction house prices | ... prices, ... prices forever, ... all realms, ... classic |
| wow ah price | wow ah prices, ... price checker, ... prices forever, ... price history, ... price tracker |
| wow forever auction | ... auction house, ... auction house prices |
| prix hv wow | prix hv wow, prix hv wow forever, prix hotel des ventes wow |

On Google Play:
- "wow auction house" and "wow ah price tracker" return mostly real-estate and auction apps; the WoW result near the
  top is "Auction House Tracker for WoW" (100+ downloads).
- Another app is named "Goblin Ledger - WoW AH Tracker" (`com.utilia.wow_ah`, 100+ downloads).
- "wow forever" returns companion, codex and guide apps; none of the names mentions auctions or prices.

## Title

Unchanged: **Goblin Ledger: WoW Forever AH** (29 of 30).

- The full primary keyword cannot fit beside the brand ("Goblin Ledger: " leaves 15 characters).
- It already holds "WoW", "Forever" and "AH".
- "Goblin Ledger: WoW AH Prices" would fit, but sits closer to the other "Goblin Ledger - WoW AH Tracker" and drops
  "Forever", the clearest difference between the two.
